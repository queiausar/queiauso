Decision, embeddings and rerankers

Three kinds of models that do not write text: decision models choose or score, embeddings find what is similar and rerankers order it.

DecisionEmbeddingsRerankersFor the data nerds

Decision

Models that do not write text: they pick an option, answer yes or no, or give a score. Useful for filtering, labelling or matching.

Search usually combines three pieces: an embedding finds candidates, a reranker orders them and a decision model chooses. Embeddings and rerankers have their own sections below.
