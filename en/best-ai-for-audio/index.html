Clone a voice

Making the AI speak with a person's voice from a recording. There is no independent ranking for cloning, so we give no score: these are well-known options that allow it.

  • ElevenLabs Voice cloning on its website and via API, with verification that the voice is yours in the professional version.
  • Fish Audio Clones a voice from a short sample; it has a website and open models under a research license.
  • Chatterbox (Resemble AI) Open source (MIT license): clones a voice from a few seconds of audio on your own computer.
  • XTTS v2 (Coqui) Veteran open source: clones voices in several languages; non-commercial license.

Only with permission. Clone your own voice or that of someone who has given you written consent.

Using someone else's voice without permission can breach their image, voice and data-protection rights, and it's used in scams. Always disclose that the voice is AI-made.
